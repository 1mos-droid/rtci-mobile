Delete your RTCI Connect account

You can request deletion of your RTCI Connect account and associated personal data at any time.

From the app

  1. Sign in to RTCI Connect.
  2. Open Explore → Settings → Request account deletion.
  3. Review the details and confirm your request.

Without the app

Email ea6971684@gmail.com with the subject RTCI Connect account deletion. Include the email address associated with your account. Do not send your password or payment credentials.

What happens next

The church will verify account ownership and process the request within 30 days. Your profile, authentication account, and associated personal data will be removed. Financial records required for accounting or legal obligations may be retained as described in the privacy policy. Submitting a request does not immediately delete your account.
